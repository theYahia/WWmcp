# Deep Research: How to Launch on Habr and VC.ru Without Getting Destroyed

## CONTEXT

We're about to publish articles about our open-source project on Habr.com and VC.ru — two main Russian-language tech platforms. This is our FIRST public appearance. We need to understand EXACTLY how to do this right, because:

1. **Habr is brutal.** Bad articles get mass-downvoted, the author's karma drops, and they can't post again. The community hates self-promotion, marketing speak, and shallow content. They love deep technical content, honest stories, and useful tools.

2. **VC.ru is different.** More business/startup focused. Tolerates promotional content better but values real numbers, real traction, and honest stories.

3. **Our project:** 114 MCP servers for non-Western APIs (Russia, CIS, Turkey, Gulf, LATAM, Africa, SE Asia), published on npm under @theyahia. 40 AI skills. 910+ tools. GitHub: theYahia/russian-mcp. This is a solo developer project (important context for Habr — they love solo devs).

4. **Risk:** If we come across as "look at my 114 vibe-coded wrappers" — we'll be destroyed. If we come across as "I solved a real problem and here's how" — we'll get stars and users.
